# Post-Hackathon Roadmap

## 0–30 days: reliability

- Harden proof worker, retries, observability, and evidence export.
- Add source-adapter interface and one additional supported chain only after ChainInfo validation.
- Commission contract review and threat-model update.

## 30–90 days: platform wedge

- Tournament-platform API and webhooks.
- Multi-match/season accounting and operator roles.
- Configurable prize tokens and fee policy on supported testnets.
- Public verifier and embeddable claim page.

## 3–12 months: commercial product

- Game-studio adapters, league dashboards, dispute evidence bundles, and operator analytics.
- Audited mainnet deployment after Attestcoin/chain readiness review.
- Enterprise custody/compliance integrations handled outside the protocol core.

## Expansion risks

- More source chains increase decoder and attestation operational surface.
- Platform-scale payouts require audits, legal review, and abuse controls.
- Do not market “trustless gameplay”; market “cryptographically evidenced settlement of a registered result.”
