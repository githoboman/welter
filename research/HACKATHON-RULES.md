# BUIDL CTC 2026 Fall — Rules and Timeline

Research snapshot: 2026-09-08. `FACT`, `INFERENCE`, and `UNKNOWN` are used deliberately.

## Current official status

**FACT — event:** BUIDL CTC 2026 Fall, “BUIDL For The Real World,” is a virtual hackathon sponsored by Creditcoin and Credit Labs. The official page lists a $15,000 prize pool: $10,000 grand prize, $3,000 second prize, and $2,000 third prize.

**FACT — deadline:** the deadline was extended to **September 13, 2026 at 23:59:00 Eastern Time**. DoraHacks’ machine-rendered event timeline represents the same instant as **September 14, 2026 at 03:59 UTC**. In Lagos this is **September 14, 2026 at 04:59 WAT**. Because “ET” is daylight time on that date, UTC-4 is the consistent conversion.

**FACT — other dates:** submissions opened August 13, 2026; the online AMA occurred August 18; winners are scheduled for September 20.

**FACT — participation snapshot:** the DoraHacks header showed 250 hackers and 67 BUIDLs when inspected. Counts are dynamic.

## Eligibility

Every team member must:

- have no criminal record or pending criminal cases;
- not be a resident of a sanctioned country;
- not be a sanctioned individual; and
- be legally permitted to participate under applicable local law.

Minimum team size is one. The official page does not state a maximum team size.

## Project rules

A qualifying project must:

- be original work created during the hackathon;
- be deployed on a testnet;
- integrate Attestcoin Protocol as a core feature;
- contain working Attestcoin integration code;
- document the technical setup and explain the integration; and
- respect third-party intellectual-property rights.

Submitted information must be truthful, and teams must own or have rights to all submitted code, content, and materials.

## Five tracks

| Track | Official fit | Useful hashtags/examples from the official track page |
|---|---|---|
| DeFi | Lending, trading, liquidity, or yield on Creditcoin | Perpetuals, derivatives, bridges, liquid staking |
| RWA | Tokenize, manage, or finance real-world assets | Tokenization, treasury, stablecoin, real estate, commodities |
| DePIN | Cross-chain data for hardware/sensor incentives, settlement, or coordination | IoT, space, telecom, storage, sensors |
| Gaming | Games or infrastructure with economies, asset ownership, or player marketplaces | In-game crypto, social platforms |
| AI | AI applications that consume cryptographically verified cross-chain data and autonomously inform decisions or trigger transactions | AI agents, on-chain decisioning, verified data |

The project may span categories, but it should nominate the one track whose user problem and core loop are clearest.

## Attestcoin requirement

**FACT:** every submission must leverage Attestcoin. The integration must be meaningful and functional. The official rules explicitly say that depth of Attestcoin use is a core scoring criterion.

**INFERENCE:** merely importing an SDK, showing a proof in the UI, or verifying a transaction without making the verified result load-bearing is unlikely to satisfy the spirit of the requirement. A strong submission should make a protected state transition impossible without a valid proof.

## Benefits beyond prizes

The top three teams enter an accelerated Creditcoin Ecosystem Investment Program process at the due-diligence stage. CEIP may provide initial investment, possible follow-on funding or grants, engineering/product advice, and access to partners and VCs. Winning teams also receive CertiK benefits listed as 8K repository-audit credits and three months of Skynet Boost; CMC listing support is excluded.
