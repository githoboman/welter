# Creditcoin Ecosystem

## Verified positioning

Creditcoin presents itself as an EVM-compatible Layer 1 and the host/control plane for Attestcoin Smart Contracts. Its current public narrative has evolved from on-chain credit/RWA infrastructure into a general-purpose cross-chain application layer.

Verified ecosystem facts published by Creditcoin include:

- EVM compatibility with Solidity, Vyper, MetaMask, Remix, Hardhat, Foundry-compatible workflows, and Ethereum-style RPCs;
- Nominated Proof-of-Stake consensus beneath the EVM layer;
- Attestcoin/previously USC live as the cross-chain capability;
- claims of 5M+ on-chain loan transactions and $100M+ loans recorded;
- a 60,000+ ecosystem community;
- a $10M CEIP investment program; and
- builder distribution through Discord, PenguinBase, and PenguinSwap.

These headline metrics are official self-reported figures, not independently audited in this research.

## Strategic direction

**FACT:** the docs call Creditcoin an RWA protocol transformed into an EVM-compatible L1, then extended with Universal Smart Contracts/Attestcoin. The website now emphasizes “deploy once, read every chain,” proof rather than bridges/oracles, and Creditcoin as a cross-chain dApp execution hub.

**FACT:** formal hackathon tracks are DeFi, RWA, DePIN, Gaming, and AI. The broader main website currently displays DeFi, RWA, DePIN, and Gaming; the hackathon adds AI explicitly.

**INFERENCE:** the ecosystem wants third-party applications that create demand for Creditcoin blockspace, validate Attestcoin as a general-purpose primitive, and have enough product substance to enter CEIP—not one-off bridge demos.

## What builders appear to be rewarded for

- Keeping important business state and policy on Creditcoin.
- Using Attestcoin as a load-bearing verified-data path.
- Reusing established EVM tooling to reduce onboarding cost.
- Showing an actual business flow in finance, RWA, physical infrastructure, games, or autonomous systems.
- Building something that can be funded, launched to the Creditcoin community, and distributed through ecosystem surfaces.
- Producing strong public evidence, because the ecosystem’s core promise is verifiability.

## What not to infer

- Creditcoin’s historic credit focus does not mean every winning project must be a lending product.
- Marketing statements that readability can eventually cover any observable chain do not mean every chain is supported on today’s public testnet. The current environment table is authoritative at build time.
- A CEIP fast-track is not guaranteed investment.
- EVM compatibility does not remove Creditcoin-specific gas estimation, precompile, attestation, or operational considerations.
